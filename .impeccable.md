## Design Context

### Users
Potential collaborators, colleagues, and anyone who encounters 4BV.ai — likely healthcare professionals, technologists, or people curious about who's behind the work. They arrive with little context and should leave with a clear impression of values and competence.

### Brand Personality
**Curious, contemplative, purposeful.**
- Curious: always exploring, building, learning — a physician who codes
- Contemplative: rooted in Buddhist philosophy (Four Brahma Viharas), thinks before acting
- Purposeful: nothing wasted, everything intentional — from the name to the work

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Tone:** Minimal but not empty. High-craft. Linear.app-level polish with philosophical quiet.
- **Theme:** Light — this is a presence site, viewed in any context, should feel open and inviting
- **Logo:** Three interlocking blue circles/rings (cornflower → steel → navy). The logo IS the design.
- **Color:** Derived from the logo's blue palette. No purple gradients, no startup neon.
- **Typography:** One distinctive pairing. Not generic. Should feel considered.
- **Motion:** Subtle. One well-crafted entrance, not scattered animations.
- **Reference:** linear.app for craft level. Stripe's sunrise for "a single memorable visual moment."
- **Anti-references:** SaaS landing pages, startup templates, long scrolling marketing sites, corporate consulting sites. This is a business card, not a pitch deck.

### Design Principles
1. **The logo speaks** — let the interlocking circles carry the visual weight
2. **Hint, don't explain** — the philosophy is present but not narrated
3. **Economy of elements** — fewer things, each perfect
4. **One memorable moment** — a single visual detail someone remembers
5. **Trust through restraint** — quality is communicated by what's left out
